Privacy Policy

Game: Coin Kitty (Kumbara Kedi in Turkish)

Effective date: 17 November 2026 (2026-11-17)

This policy explains how the game (the "game", "we", "us") handles information. The short version: the game does not collect any personal data from anyone. If you turn on optional cloud save, a copy of your progress is kept in your own Google Play Games or Apple Game Center account, which we cannot see.

1. Information we collect

None. The game has no account, no login and no sign-up of its own. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. It does not create an advertising ID or any other identifier for you. You may type a name for the hero of the game (for example instead of "Richie"); that name stays in the save file on your device (section 2) and is never sent anywhere.

2. Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves a small file on your device only. It contains things like:

  • your best scores and Daily Jar results,
  • your pocket money (an in-game amount, not real money), room decorations and unlocked jar skins,
  • your settings, such as language and sound.
  • the hero name you typed, if you changed it.

This file never leaves your device through the game, and we cannot see or access it. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the game or by clearing the game's data in your device settings. Deleting it also resets your progress.

Depending on your device settings, your phone or tablet may include app data in its own backups (for example, Google or iCloud backups). Those backups are handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies, not by us.

3. Offline play, third parties and optional cloud save

The game plays fully offline. By itself it sends no data over the internet, and it contains no third-party services or SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, payments or social login. The Daily Jar is generated on your device from the date, so it also works offline.

Optional cloud save. On Android phones and tablets and on iPhone and iPad, where your version of the game offers it, the game can keep a copy of your progress in your own Google Play Games account (Android) or in your Apple Game Center account, which keeps it in your iCloud (iPhone, iPad). It is off until a grown-up turns it on in Settings > Cloud save > Connect, after a short times-table question (a parental gate). Then:

  • What is copied: best scores, pocket money (an in-game amount, not real money), things bought, jar looks and outfits, the background theme and the hero name you typed. Your language and sound settings and an unfinished game stay on the device.
  • The copy lives in your own Google or Apple account. We cannot see or access it. Google (Google Play Games Services) and Apple (Game Center, iCloud) handle it, and the sign-in that comes with it (for example your Play Games player ID), under their own privacy policies. Google Play Games Services may also process information about how you use the game, as described in Google's privacy policy.
  • Settings > Cloud save > Disconnect stops the copying on that device. The game keeps working offline whether cloud save is on or off.

4. Sharing your result

When you tap Share, the game draws a picture of your score, with a snapshot of your jar at the end of the game, and prepares a short text (with a link to the game once it is in the app store). Nothing is sent by the game itself:

  • Save image stores that picture on your device only (in your Pictures folder on a computer).
  • Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, X, Facebook, LinkedIn and Other apps open another app or website with the text and picture; on a computer the picture is saved in your Pictures folder for you to add and the text is put on your clipboard. Because this leaves the game, a grown-up first answers a short times-table question (a parental gate).

You decide whether to send anything, and where. If you send it through another app or website, that service's own privacy policy applies.

5. App stores

You download the game from Google Play or the Apple App Store. Google and Apple process information about your download and device under their own privacy policies. They may also give developers anonymous, aggregated statistics (such as install counts or crash rates) through their developer consoles. These statistics do not identify you, and we do not combine them with any other information.

6. Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children. It has no ads, no purchases, no chat and no way to contact other players. Cloud save stays off until a grown-up turns it on (section 3); children's accounts are managed by their parents through Google or Apple, and if an account may not use it, the game simply plays offline.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. Your saved progress is entirely under your control on your device (see section 2). If you used cloud save, you can delete the copy in the Play Games settings on Android (delete this game's data) or in the iCloud storage settings on iPhone and iPad, and stop it at any time with Disconnect (section 3). You are still welcome to contact us with any question.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example ads, analytics, purchases or online features), we will update this policy before that version is released, change the effective date above and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first.
